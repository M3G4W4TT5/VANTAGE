# Provider inventory assessment and proposed spec additions

> Reviewed 2026-09-21 against PROTOTYPE_SPEC v0.4; all six priority additions are required by explicit user instruction in v0.6. NWS, OpenAQ, BC Open511, Earth Search, EONET and Cloudflare Radar are required DS-23–28. Other candidates below remain unadopted. No live connector smoke tests were performed.

The [complete inventory](source-inventory-2026-09-21.md) preserves all 896 supplied rows and links to the original attachment. The [current specification](../PROTOTYPE_SPEC.md) already covers many of the useful domains. The [earlier review](source-candidate-review.md) records the previous source selection and its adopted changes.

## Adopted recommendation

The specification now includes official alerts, air-quality measurements and regional road incidents. These introduce useful map information that existing weather forecasts, earthquake/fire observations and vehicle tracks do not cover. NWS, OpenAQ and BC Open511 are required reference connectors. Earth Search, EONET and Cloudflare Radar are also required reference connectors. Avoid adopting hundreds of publishers or duplicating every existing tracking provider.

The source list is useful discovery material, but it is not a list of 896 usable data integrations. Supplied labels mix data APIs, news feeds, stream hosts, software dependencies and infrastructure. “Structured” does not establish a schema or reuse permission, and a source-code reference does not establish current availability. Documentation checks below establish promising integration paths, not approved licences for every operation or working integrations.

## Already covered or better handled as configuration

- **News and RSS:** Most publishers belong in a curated DS-13 feed catalog. Validate actual feed URLs and permissions; keep publisher, language, country/topic and provenance. Do not bundle every entry or fetch them all by default. Syndication through Google or another transport must retain the original publisher, and duplicated articles are not independent evidence. Full article crawling remains outside DS-13.
- **Aircraft, vessels, satellites and transit:** Additional ADS-B vendors, OpenSky, Wingbits, TTC and regional feeds are alternatives or instances of existing connectors. Compare coverage, quotas and rights before adding provider-specific code. No need for a second tracking architecture. A Fintraffic entry marked “not-currently-wired” in the supplied list says nothing about VANTAGE's required Digitraffic connector.
- **Weather and imagery:** Additional Open-Meteo endpoints extend DS-10 only where their semantics fit. Historical reanalysis is modelled data, not automatically a station observation. NASA imagery and other known sources should map to existing DS entries rather than duplicated domains.
- **Maps, geocoding and knowledge:** OSM, Wikidata, Wikimedia and geocoders may enrich existing search/infrastructure contracts. Verify exact services and usage policies before adoption; do not assume public geocoders permit bulk requests or autocomplete. A generic ArcGIS, GitHub or cloud-storage domain needs an exact dataset owner and path.
- **Cybersecurity feeds:** AbuseIPDB, OTX, URLhaus and similar sources are conditional future enrichment, not a reason to expand public-IP lookup into a full threat platform. Preserve dated allegations, source confidence and indicator type; never infer exact physical locations.
- **Markets, macroeconomics, tenders, filings and research:** World Bank, IMF, OECD, ECB, FRED, SEC, TED, SAM, USASpending, arXiv and patent sources can support later apps. Retain them in the inventory; do not add entire finance, procurement or research dashboards to ATLAS. Pizzint or other proxy indicators do not independently establish a military/conflict event.
- **Provider status pages:** Can inform connector health where useful. They are statements about a provider's own service, separate from measured source freshness and regional internet outages. A deployment platform such as `railway.instatus.com` is not railway transport data.

## Entries that should not become source requirements

Exclude the following roles from a content-source catalog unless a separate concrete use is identified:

- Login/OAuth, billing and outbound communication: Google account sign-in, Clerk, WorkOS, DodoPayments test/live, Resend, Discord OAuth and Telegram notification APIs. Their presence does not authorise messages or require these services.
- Telemetry and diagnostics: Axiom, Sentry, DebugBear and World Monitor analytics endpoints. VANTAGE keeps no third-party telemetry by default.
- Application infrastructure: World Monitor's own proxies, maps, API and deployment endpoints; shared Supabase/storage/CDN hosts. Source-app implementation dependencies and paid services do not transfer into the approved VANTAGE stack.
- Schemas and developer references: W3C/PURL/schema namespaces, documentation and libraries. They may describe data formats; they are not data feeds.
- Test placeholders and local hosts: localhost, loopback IPs, example domains, `internal.example.com`, `your-app.convex.site` and `worldmonitor.invalid`. Never promote these into outbound source configuration.
- AI/search/crawling infrastructure: model providers, Exa, Brave and Firecrawl are optional tools/services with separate costs and permissions, not automatically public data sources.
- Streaming/CDN hosts without a verified original broadcaster/operator and permitted playback path. Technical reachability alone does not establish embedding, caching or recording rights.

Keep these rows in the archival inventory so future reviewers can trace the original list; exclusion from the app is not deletion of the evidence.

## Contract requirements adopted for the selected additions

Use existing versioned source-specific properties and shared contracts where possible. The following semantics are reflected in the specification for required connectors and apply to optional adapters when implemented; fixtures remain implementation work:

1. **Alerts and incidents:** Stable provider IDs, revision/reference relationships, issued/updated/effective/expiry times, active/cancelled/ended states and preserved original geometry. Removal from a “current” feed does not prove the incident ended. Test updates, cancellations, missing geometry, scheduled closures and late arrivals.
2. **Environmental measurements:** Quantity, unit, averaging interval, station/sensor, quality flags and observation versus retrieval time. Test missing values, unit differences, stale stations and provider revisions; do not silently manufacture AQI.
3. **Reports and derived events:** Preserve document identity, publisher, publication time, original source references, event-time bounds, location precision and derivation method. Country-level mentions remain country-level. Preserve shared lineage across aggregators to prevent false corroboration.
4. **Aggregate indicators/outages:** Geographic or ASN scope, reporting interval, method and cause/confidence attribution. Support appropriate tables/areas rather than fabricated point locations; separate provider status from internet conditions.
5. **Imagery discovery:** Collection/item identity, footprint, acquisition interval, available assets/bands, cloud metadata, access terms and processing lineage. Distinguish catalog search from renderable assets and bound asset downloads/cache costs.

All additions inherit backend credential handling, cancellation, bounded demand, provenance, history/replay limits and per-operation rights. A free API does not imply permission to export its payload or archive linked media.

## Verification and remaining uncertainty

- All 896 supplied rows are retained in order. The raw copy is byte-identical to the attachment; the Markdown table preserves both original fields and adds row numbers.
- Every row was considered during role/domain triage. This document groups decisions; it does not claim an individual licence audit of every publisher or host.
- Linked primary documentation was checked for the shortlisted providers. Public documentation/catalog access was checked, not authenticated end-to-end connector operation. Quotas and terms must be revalidated when implementing.
- The originating World Monitor code tree was not supplied or audited. The attachment provides no repository revision or extraction timestamp; code paths and “+N more” counts remain unverified source claims.
- No paid services, accounts, credentials, subscriptions or external messages were created. PROTOTYPE_SPEC.md and DESIGN.md were subsequently updated following explicit user adoption; this remains documentation work, not integration verification.
