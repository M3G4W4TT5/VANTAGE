## Edit prompt

Edit this existing Vantage logo with one typographic change: align the diagonals of the capital V with the diagonal edges of the three-triangle emblem.
The emblem's triangle sides are approximately 60 degrees to the horizontal baseline. The existing V is too narrow and steep. Redraw ONLY the capital V with a wider opening: its left descending stroke must be parallel to the outer right sloping edge of the RIGHTMOST upright triangle, and its right ascending stroke must be parallel to the left sloping edge of that triangle. Both V arms should therefore be approximately 60 degrees to the horizontal, with a symmetric 60-degree inner angle. This must be a clearly visible widening of the V, not a subtle change. Preserve the existing capital height, baseline, white stroke weight and clean sans-serif treatment, with a small flat base at its bottom joint.
Preserve the three-triangle emblem exactly: same shape, dimensions, rounded corners, gaps and position. Preserve the lowercase letters "antage" exactly in their current typeface and weight. Allow only the minimal horizontal repositioning of these letters needed for natural kerning after the widened V. Keep the text spelled exactly "Vantage".
Maintain the single horizontal logo composition, solid white artwork and solid black background. Crisp flat edges. No glow, light, shadows, texture, extra text, angle labels, construction lines or decorative elements.
