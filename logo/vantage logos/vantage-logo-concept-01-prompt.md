# Vantage logo concept 01

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Raster concept based on the user's three-triangle draft and Palantir typography reference.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a pristine flat digital logo image from scratch on a solid black canvas, wide horizontal composition, like a professionally typeset brand identity.
The exact single word is "Vantage": uppercase V, lowercase antage. Large clean white sans-serif typography, regular to medium weight, elegant and highly readable, inspired by the restrained simple typography of Palantir. A double-storey a and a single-storey g, carefully kerned letters, generous gap between icon and name. The word must be perfectly spelled and its white strokes solid and uninterrupted.
To the left of the word is this precise white geometric emblem: THREE separate rounded equilateral triangles forming a broad symmetric cluster. The middle triangle is elevated and points DOWN, with a horizontal flat upper edge. The left and right triangles sit lower and point UP, with aligned flat bottom edges. The left side triangle's upper point is near the middle triangle's upper left corner; the right side triangle's upper point is near its upper right corner. The inverted central triangle nests between the side triangles with two crisp parallel-sided black diagonal channels dividing the three shapes. All three triangles use the same gentle corner radius, perfectly smooth outlines, pure white solid interiors, no holes. Their shapes never touch. This must retain the user's draft's three-triangle configuration.
The emblem height is close to the full height of the wordmark including the g descender. The emblem width is about 1.65 times its own height. The gap between emblem and wordmark is about one half of the emblem's height. The name is the dominant horizontal element.
One single horizontal logo lockup centered on a black field, generous clear margins all around. Sharp mathematically smooth shapes and clear typography. Flat solid black and white only. No texture, distress, grain, decoration, surface effects, outlines, thin scratches, extraneous marks, mockups, shadows, gradients, captions, or additional logos.

## Final refinement prompt

Edit this Vantage logo image: remove the entire glowing light halo and bloom effect. Make the background uniformly pure black right up to every shape and letter. Make the three triangle shapes and the word "Vantage" flat solid white with sharp clean edges. Restore the black interior counters in the letters a, a, g and e so the word is perfectly legible. Keep the same logo geometry, layout, typography, scale and positioning. A plain two-color graphic suitable for printing: white ink on black paper. No lighting effects at all.
